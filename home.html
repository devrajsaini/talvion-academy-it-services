FAQs

Everything you need to know about Talvion programs, admission process, and placement support.

What makes Talvion programs different from other online courses?

Talvion started as an IT company shipping software for real clients. Students train on the exact same build, test, and ship workflows we use on client work — not dummy classroom exercises. Live classes, real deadlines, and 1-on-1 mentorship.

What kind of placement support and hiring network do you provide?

Classes are not the finish line. We provide end-to-end placement support including resume optimization, portfolio packaging, mock technical and HR interviews, and direct referrals across our 50+ hiring partner network.

Are easy EMI or monthly installment options available?

Yes! We believe finances should never stop learning. We offer zero-cost monthly EMI options starting from ₹2,000 / month with instant admission and flexible approval.

Can beginners or students from non-IT backgrounds join?

Absolutely. Every track starts from foundational concepts and builds up to advanced production standards. No prior coding or computer science degree is required.
